## 3. Full Description (Google Play & App Store)

Never wonder how to stand, look, or position your arms in photographs again.

CamAssis is your personal AI photography director. Combining curated professional photography poses with an intuitive transparent camera guide, CamAssis helps anyone look confident, natural, and aesthetic in front of the lens.

### KEY FEATURES

📸 GHOST POSE CAMERA
Overlay transparent reference guides directly over your live camera preview. Adjust opacity from 10% to 90%, choose between original photograph or skeletal outline, and line up your shot with pixel-perfect accuracy.

🧠 REAL-TIME AI POSE COACH
Our on-device 33-point anatomical pose engine detects body angles (head, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees) and provides instant directional feedback until your alignment glows green.

✨ HUNDREDS OF CURATED POSES
Explore aesthetic poses organized by mood, setting, and style:
• Beach Days & Golden Hour
• Aesthetic Mirror Selfies & Streetwear
• Romantic Couples & Candid Moments
• Travel Viewpoints & Sitting Poses
• Editorial Fashion & Full-Body Silhouettes

🎨 "MY POSE" — CREATE CUSTOM POSE GUIDES
Saw a pose you love on Instagram, Pinterest, or your camera roll? Import any photo, and CamAssis will automatically extract its body landmarks into a reusable transparent camera overlay.

🔒 ABSOLUTE PRIVACY GUARANTEE
Your photos belong solely to you. CamAssis automatically saves captured photos directly to your native device Gallery ("CamAssis" album). We never upload, process, or store your personal photos on cloud servers. All AI landmark matching runs locally on your device.

Enjoy photography with confidence. Download CamAssis today!
