# CamAssis Legal Documentation

## Part 1: Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026

CamAssis ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how your personal information is handled when using the CamAssis mobile application.

### 1. The Core CamAssis Privacy Guarantee
- **Zero Cloud Photo Storage**: Photographs captured using CamAssis are immediately saved directly to your device's native photo storage (Photos on iOS, Gallery on Android). We do not operate a remote photo server, and your photographs are never transmitted to our backend.
- **On-Device AI Processing**: Pose landmark extraction and posture alignment coaching run locally on your device. We never stream live camera feeds or frame buffers to third-party AI APIs.
- **No Pose Overlay in Captures**: The transparent pose reference is strictly a live preview guide; captured files contain solely your camera sensor's high-resolution capture.

### 2. Information We Collect
- **Optional Account Data**: If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and full name to manage preferences and saved pose favorites.
- **Guest Usage**: You may use CamAssis entirely as a Guest without creating an account or providing an email address.
- **Session Metadata**: Minimal anonymous operational statistics (e.g. number of poses saved locally).

### 3. Permissions Requested
- **Camera (`NSCameraUsageDescription` / `CAMERA`)**: Required to display the camera preview and transparent pose overlay.
- **Photo Library (`NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**: Required to save captured photos directly to your device's gallery and import custom pose photos.

### 4. Account and Data Deletion
In compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and global privacy regulations, users can permanently delete their account and associated metadata at any time by navigating to **Profile -> Delete Account**.
